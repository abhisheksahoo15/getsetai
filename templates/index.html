Frequently Asked Questions

Can’t find your answer? Reach us at support@getsetai.in

What does GetSetAI Innovations do?

We design, fine-tune, and deploy AI/ML models for businesses and developers. From NLP to Computer Vision and Agents, our goal is to make AI deployment as seamless as possible.

Do you offer pre-built AI models?

Yes. You can explore our growing library of pre-trained models for text, vision, speech, and more. These can be used out-of-the-box or customized for your specific use case.

Can I integrate my own data or API keys?

Absolutely. You can bring your own dataset for fine-tuning and even use your API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, or open-source frameworks) inside our platform.

How does model deployment work?

We provide one-click deployment to cloud or on-premise environments. Every commit is built, versioned, and can be rolled back instantly—so your models are always production-ready.

Is there a free plan or trial?

Yes. We offer limited free credits so you can try building and deploying models. For startups and enterprises, we also provide custom subscription plans.
