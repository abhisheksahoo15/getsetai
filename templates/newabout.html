Our Journey

It all started with a dream — a group of curious minds staying up till 3AM training their first models, burning GPUs, debugging Python scripts, and sipping chai. That spark turned into a movement — GetSetAI. We are not just another tech club; we are a living, breathing AI tribe that collaborates, builds, breaks, and builds again.

From small-town engineering students to industry pros in top MNCs, we are a united front redefining how AI education and innovation happen in this country. Each hackathon, each project, each failure — teaches us more than any textbook ever could.

We host national-level events, build real-world ML pipelines, deploy apps on Azure, AWS, GCP — and connect people with mentors who actually care. No boring PPTs, no theoretical nonsense, only practical, hands-on, crazy AI hustle!

The Road Ahead

Imagine a future where a village kid in Bihar or a student in remote Kerala can deploy cutting-edge AI models with a single click. Where innovation isn't limited by geography. That is our vision.

We want to create 10,000+ young ML engineers who can build the next ChatGPT, the next Tesla-like system, or even the next cure for complex diseases.
